# Getting started

Your first trip into the grid, from a fresh survival world. For what you do once you're there, see
[progression.md](progression.md); for the full feature list, [features.md](features.md).

## 1. Gather Luminessence and bits

Luminessence is the grid's currency and the base of most recipes. In the real world:

1. Mine down below **Y 20**. Luminance Ore generates there, in stone and deepslate.
2. Each ore also gives up 0–2 loose **bits** (Binary 0 or Binary 1), unless you mine it with Silk Touch. You need
   eight Binary 1s for the laser's Pixels, and any eight bits make a Byte.
3. Craft a **Luminessence Detector** if you want help: right-click a block to detect ore below it.
4. Bring a stack or two. You'll want it for the laser, for befriending a Bool, and for trading.

## 2. Build a Digitizing Laser

```
 L D L        L  Luminessence      E  Eye of Ender
 P E P        D  Diamond           I  Iron Block
 I I I        P  Pixel (4 Pixel Dust; Pixel Dust is a Binary 1 and a Luminessence)
```

Place it anywhere in the real world. Optionally craft a **Laser Tuner** (a compass, two iron ingots and a
Luminessence) to choose where it sends you later.

## 3. Prepare for the trip

Dying in the grid normally sends you back to the real world with your items left behind. Before you go:

- **Memory Paging** (a Totem of Undying, Bytes and Luminessence) respawns you at your last terminal.
- A **Core Dump** (Memory Paging, netherite, echo shards, Luminessence) also keeps your inventory.
- An **Escape Key** (ender pearls, diamonds, an echo shard) takes you out of the grid from anywhere, once.
- Armor and a weapon. The city center is safe, but patrols and corrupted programs aren't.

## 4. Digitize

Right-click the laser and **stay within 4 blocks** for two seconds while it scans. You arrive at the
**City Terminal**, in the City Core at the center of the default grid.

## 5. First steps in the grid

- **Find the Bool.** One floats on the avenue just north of the City Terminal. Feed it Luminessence until hearts
  show (each piece has a 1 in 3 chance). It follows you, turns red near danger, and shows a yellow diamond when you
  face a stronghold. Right-click it empty-handed to make it sit or follow.
- **Look around the City Core.** It's free of the regime and of decay: a safe base. Programs wander the streets.
- **Find the garage.** Somewhere in the City Core is a lit bay with a wide door, and the **Mechanic** inside. It
  sells Boost Blocks, Light Barrier Emitters, Coal Dust and a **Lightcycle Baton** for Luminessence.
- **Turn on F3's grid section** (`digitalfrontier:grid`, via F3+F6) if you want to read the ground: decay, the
  regime's control, and your alignment.

## 6. Getting home

- Walk or ride far out to the **Portal**, a beam of light at the grid's edge, and step in. It starts sealed: the
  first player to step in holding an **Escape Key** spends it, and from then on the Portal is open to everyone. The
  **Portal Keeper** there sells keys.
- Or use an **Escape Key** anywhere.

Either way you wake up where you last slept in the real world. Next time, your laser (with a Laser Tuner) can send you
to any terminal you've discovered, not just the city.
