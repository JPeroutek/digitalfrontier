# TRON characters

A reference catalog of the TRON franchise's characters, for designing the mod's content. It is built from the
[TRON Wiki](https://tron.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Characters)'s Characters category (210 pages, read November
2026). The summaries are our own words. See `docs/character-ideas.md` for the mod additions they suggest.

**Names in the mod.** The mod uses its own names (The Sovereign, The Warden) and leaves the films' names to
datapacks and resource packs (see `docs/lieutenants.md`). New content should do the same: draw on what a character
*does*, not their name or likeness.

**Terms used below:**
- **User:** a human in the real world. Most programs look like the User who wrote them.
- **Basic:** a program someone wrote.
- **ISO** (isomorphic algorithm): a program that arose on its own from the Grid's "Sea of Simulation".
- **Circuitry colour:** signals allegiance. Blue or white means free or loyal to the Users; red, orange or yellow
  means the regime. Turning a program to the regime is called *repurposing*.
- **Derezzed:** deleted, shattering into cubes.

## TRON (1982): the ENCOM mainframe

**Programs:**

| Character | Role | What they do | Mod hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tron** | hero | A security program written by Alan Bradley, devoted to the Users. Captured and forced into the Game Grid, he becomes its disc-arena master. He returns in Legacy and Uprising, where Clu repurposes him into Rinzler. | the friendly champion; a "restored" form of the duelist lieutenant |
| **Clu** (original) | hacker | Flynn's hacker program, in his likeness, with yellow circuitry. Derezzed by the MCP while searching the system in a tank. | tanks; infiltration |
| **Master Control Program (MCP)** | final villain | A rogue control program that rules the mainframe. It absorbs other programs to grow, and works with Ed Dillinger. Seen as a giant spinning column with a face. | **final boss**: a stationary system core |
| **Sark** | commander | The MCP's commander, in Dillinger's likeness. Runs the Game Grid from a giant carrier and fights with a disc. | a commander lieutenant on a flying carrier |
| **Sark's Lieutenant** | aide | Sark's protocol and intelligence officer, belittled by him. Left behind to guard prisoners. | a weak officer mob that flees and raises the alarm |
| **Dumont** | Tower Guardian | Guards the last free I/O Tower, the only place programs can talk to their Users. An old program in Walter Gibbs's likeness. | a guardian NPC at the Portal or terminals |
| **Yori** | builder and pilot | Written by Lora; runs the digitizing simulations and pilots the Solar Sailer. | Solar Sailer transit; the digitizing laser |
| **Ram** | conscript | An actuarial program captured for the games. He befriends Tron and dies after the light cycle escape. | captive programs to free |
| **Crom** | conscript | A compound-interest banking program, forced into the games for believing in the Users. Fights with a cesta. | the ring game; captives |
| **Bit** | companion | A floating polyhedron that can only say "yes" or "no". | **a companion pet** |
| **Guards** | soldiers | The MCP's red security programs, armed with stun pikes. | enforcers |
| **Monk programs** | inhabitants | Robed data-pusher programs. | ambient city programs |
| **Voice of the Grid** | announcer | The disembodied voice that makes announcements across the system. | a grid-wide announcer for events |

**Users:**
- **Kevin Flynn:** game designer and hacker; later the Grid's creator, trapped inside it for 21 years.
- **Alan Bradley:** wrote Tron; Flynn's friend and Sam's guardian.
- **Lora Baines-Bradley:** wrote Yori, built the digitizing laser with Gibbs, and is Jet's mother.
- **Walter Gibbs:** ENCOM's founder.
- **Ed Dillinger:** stole Flynn's games, partnered with the MCP and wrote Sark.
- **Peter:** Dillinger's assistant, and the User of Sark's Lieutenant.
- **Roy Kleinberg:** wrote Ram.
- **Mr. Henderson:** a savings-and-loan manager who wrote Crom.

**Users and others:**

- **Others:** Grandma and Grandpa Flynn; Marv the dog; Ernie (a security guard); Jackie Jones and Dr. Springer

**Minor programs (Battle Grids and Legacy background):**

  Hamilton, Hue, Kalev, Necker, Pythia, Tarski, Tasck, Theseus, Weema, Zarek, Radix, and an unnamed female program.

- **The Destitute Program** is a program with no function, shown homeless. It suggests a down-and-out kind of
  ambient program.

## TRON: Identity and TRON: Catalyst (2023, 2025): the Arq Grid
A separate grid, where the faction **Core** governs.
- **The city:** Vertical Slice.
- **The Repository:** a library holding the grid's history.
- **The Outlands:** the wilderness around the city.
- **The glitch:** a destructive anomaly in the server, causing "glitch storms".
